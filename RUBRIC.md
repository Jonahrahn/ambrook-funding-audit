# Funding Library data-quality rubric v1
Scored 2026-08-06. Today's date for all recency and deadline math: **2026-08-06**.

Every program page is scored on five criteria, 1 to 3 each. Maximum 15.
A criterion is scored from what a farmer can see on the public page. No account, no inside knowledge.

## C1. Freshness
Based on the "Last Updated" date printed on the program page.

- **3** Updated on or after 2025-08-06 (within 12 months)
- **2** Updated between 2024-08-06 and 2025-08-05 (12 to 24 months)
- **1** Updated before 2024-08-06, or no date shown at all

## C2. Deadline integrity
Based on the deadline or status text, read against 2026-08-06.

- **3** Status is internally coherent and currently true. A "Closed" date is in the past. A "Due" date is in the future. A stated rolling or continuous cycle is labelled as such.
- **2** No deadline shown and the program is genuinely rolling, continuous, or has no application window.
- **1** Any of: a "Closed" date that has not happened yet, a "Due" date that has already passed, a deadline given as month and day with no year so the reader cannot tell which cycle it belongs to, or a status that contradicts the body text.

## C3. Source traceability
Can a farmer get from this page to the authority that actually runs the program?

- **3** Links to the specific program page on the administering agency's own site
- **2** Links only to a generic agency homepage, a form, or a third party
- **1** No outbound source link

## C4. Funding specificity
- **3** A specific dollar figure, range, or a stated cost-share percentage with a cap
- **2** Qualitative only: "varies", "depends on the project", a percentage with no cap
- **1** No funding information

## C5. Eligibility completeness
Could a farmer determine whether they qualify without leaving the page?

- **3** Explicit eligibility criteria listed as discrete requirements
- **2** Eligibility described in prose or partially, with material gaps
- **1** No eligibility information

## Scoring notes
- Score what is displayed, not what is true elsewhere. C3 measures whether the page points at the truth, not whether the page agrees with it. Agreement is tested separately in the primary-source cross-check.
- A page that 404s or fails to render scores 0 and is reported as a broken entry, not scored on the five criteria.
- Ties in the report are broken by C1 then C2, because staleness and a wrong deadline are the two failures that cost a farmer money.
